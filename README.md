# Coolest Art — "Koi to Dragon"

A golden koi leaps out of dark water mid-transformation into a dragon,
its scales igniting into fire above a neon city at night. The piece is
built around one unmistakable shape — the blazing S-curve of the
fish-dragon against a deep blue skyline — so it reads clearly even at
thumbnail size, and the warm gold against cold neon gives it a single
bold mood: transformation, the old legend of the koi that becomes a dragon.

`entry.png` is a 912x608 PNG (1,034,235 bytes, within the 1 MiB cap).
`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) deterministically copies it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`; running it twice writes identical bytes.
